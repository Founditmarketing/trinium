quality home improvement & solar installation

We pride ourselves on providing quality home improvement and solar installation! Our process starts with first estimating a prospective client’s home to see if they qualify for solar. Many homes do not! We have an elite engineering team available to ensure the accuracy of our measurements. Once we gather all of this information, we are able to create an estimate; we then send out a consultant to explain and advise prospective client’s on the various options they have, and which are best for them! We are more than solar! Trinium can evaluate other aspects of your home to reduce consumption! (AC Units, water heaters, pool pumps, lights, insulation, attic fans, sealants).
